Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how the application ("App") handles user data.

Information We Collect

The App may request or use the following permissions:

  • Camera – used for capturing images or scanning functionality inside the app.
  • Location (Fine & Coarse) – used to provide location-based features.
  • Microphone – used for audio recording features when explicitly triggered by the user.
  • Storage (Read/Write External Storage) – used to save or access files chosen by the user.
  • Internet Access – used to connect to online services and APIs required for app functionality.
  • Vibration – used for user feedback and notifications.

The App does NOT collect personal data such as names, emails, or contacts unless explicitly provided by the user within app features.

How We Use Information

Permissions are used only for core app functionality and user-requested features. Data is processed locally on the device or sent securely to required services only when necessary for functionality.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share personal data with third parties. Data is only shared with external services when required for app functionality (e.g., APIs you explicitly use).

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (such as Google services or APIs) which may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
